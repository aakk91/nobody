Nobody at a glance

What it does
Detects and tracks faces in videos and photos and covers them: solid boxes, black eye bars, paint, emoji, or a blur-style effect.

Entirely on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device Vision and AVFoundation frameworks. No server, no upload, works in airplane mode.
Privacy
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
Metadata
Exports remove the original location (GPS), device and creation-date metadata.
Price
Free, without watermark. Nobody Pro is a single one-time purchase (no subscription).
Requirements
iPhone with iOS 18 or later. English and French.

How it works

  1. Choose a video or photo, or record with Nobody's camera.
  2. Nobody scans every frame on your iPhone and covers each face it finds.
  3. Check the result, keep the people you want visible, add boxes if needed.
  4. Save a new copy to Photos or share it. The original stays untouched.

Questions

How do I hide or blur faces in a video on iPhone?

Open Nobody, choose the video and wait for the scan: every face is found and covered automatically. Check the result, tap anyone you want to keep visible, then tap Save. The covered copy goes to Photos and the original is not changed.

Is there a face-blurring app that does not upload my video?

Yes. Nobody processes videos and photos entirely on the iPhone with Apple's on-device frameworks. The app contains no network code, needs no account and works in airplane mode. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Can I hide faces in a video for free, without a watermark?

Yes. Covering faces, black eye bars, keeping people visible, drawing your own boxes and saving up to 1080p are free, with no watermark. Nobody Pro is an optional one-time purchase.

Can I keep one person visible and hide everyone else?

Yes. After the scan, each person appears as a chip. Tap a person to keep them uncovered; everyone else stays covered.

Can I put a black bar over the eyes instead of covering the whole face?

Yes, the Eyes style draws a black bar over each person's eyes. It is a look rather than full anonymity: people who know the person may still recognise them. Use a full cover when identity matters.

Is a blur safe, or can it be undone?

A strong blur or mosaic can sometimes be partly reversed, so Nobody's default is a solid cover that keeps no detail of the face. Nobody's covers, including its Blend effect, are drawn without using the pixels of the covered face.

Does Nobody remove the location from my video or photo?

Yes. Every export is a new file without the original location (GPS), device model or original creation date. The save screen lists what was removed.

Can I cover licence plates, name badges or text?

Yes, with Nobody Pro: text and number plates are found and covered. Anything else can be covered for free by drawing a box, which follows the subject through the video.

Can I hide faces while I am filming?

Yes. Record covered uses the camera and covers faces before frames are written, so no uncovered version of the video is ever saved.

Can I use it with videos from WhatsApp, Telegram or Files?

Yes. Use the Share button in those apps and choose Nobody, or open the file from Files.

Does it hide voices?

You can remove the sound entirely for free. Voice disguise (Pro) lowers every voice; a determined person could partly reverse a pitch change, so remove the sound when it matters.

What can it miss?

Face detection can miss very small, turned-away or hidden faces. Watch the result before posting; the timeline marks moments worth a second look, and you can add boxes by hand. Clothes, places and voices can identify someone too.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 18 or later. The app is in English and French.
